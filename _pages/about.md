Hi! I am a fourth year PhD candidate working under the guidance of Prof. Jeffrey Fessler and Prof. Douglas Noll at the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor in the exciting field of magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) reconstruction and denoising. I completed my undergraduate studies at the University of Texas at Austin working under the supervision of Prof. Jon Tamir. My research interests include signal and image processing, inverse problems, MRI, and the intersection of machine learning with classical model-based image reconstruction. Currently, I am developing a data-driven denoising method for functional MRI using random matrix theory (RMT) to improve the signal specificity of active brain regions by removing thermal noise from the image time series.
