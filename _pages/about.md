Hi! I am a fourth year PhD candidate working under the guidance of Prof. Jeffrey Fessler and Prof. Douglas Noll at the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor in the exciting field of magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) reconstruction and denoising. I completed my Bachelor's degree at the University of Texas at Austin in ECE while beginning my exploration into MRI research under the supervision of Prof. Jon Tamir. Shortly after, I completed my Master's degree at the University of Michigan in ECE with a focus in signal processing and machine learning (ML). 

My research interests include signal and image processing, solving inverse problems, MRI, and the intersection of ML with classical model-based image reconstruction. Currently, I am developing a data-driven denoising method for functional MRI that uses random matrix theory (RMT) to remove thermal noise from image time series, improving the signal specificity to more precisely map active brain regions.
